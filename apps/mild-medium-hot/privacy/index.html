Privacy policy: Mild, Medium, Hot

Effective date: November 13, 2026

App version covered: 1.0, for iOS and Android

Developer: Popsicle Apps

Contact: hello.popsicle.apps@gmail.com

Mild, Medium, Hot helps you remember the restaurants you visit, the dishes you order and how spicy you like them. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app, on iOS and on Android. "We" means Popsicle Apps, the developer of the app.

The short version

  • What you enter is stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage, by default.
  • There is no account. The app doesn't send us your information, and we don't operate any server or cloud service for it.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party services that receive your information.
  • Your information leaves the app only if you export a backup and choose where to send or save it, or if your device's own backup or transfer features include it, depending on your settings.
  • If you email us for support, we receive what you write (see "If you contact us").

Information you enter

The app stores only what you type or choose:

  • Restaurants: name, and optionally cuisine, a location you type, notes, and whether it's a favorite.
  • Dishes: name, your spice level, and optionally a custom spice note ("what to say when ordering"), tasting notes, and whether it's a favorite.
  • The date and time each restaurant and dish was created or last changed.
  • Your appearance setting (Light, Dark or Follow System).

The "location" field is plain text that you type. The app doesn't use GPS or location services, and doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other apps' data.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Your information is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it, and we have no access to it. It stays there until you change or delete it, reset the app, or delete the app. We don't keep any copy.

Network use

The app doesn't send your restaurants, dishes, notes or settings anywhere, and doesn't contact any server operated by us or by a third party on our behalf.

  • When you tap Privacy Policy or Contact Support in the app's Settings, it opens this website in your web browser (see "This website").
  • On Android, the app lists the standard "internet" and "network state" permissions that its software components include. The app doesn't use them to send your information.

Backups you export

Settings → Export data creates a backup file containing all your restaurants and dishes, including your notes. The app then opens your device's share options, and you choose where the file goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop on iOS, or Files, Google Drive or another app on Android, or email or a messaging app on either.

  • From that point, the file is handled by the destination you chose, under its own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive a copy.
  • The backup file is not encrypted. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • While you're sharing it, the app keeps a temporary copy in its own cache and deletes it automatically shortly afterwards (or the next time the app starts). It never deletes copies you've saved elsewhere.

Importing a backup

Settings → Import backup reads only the file you pick. The app checks the file and shows what it contains before anything changes. Your existing data is replaced only after you confirm.

  • On iOS, the system gives the app a temporary copy of the file you picked, and the app deletes that copy after reading it.
  • On Android, the app reads the file you picked directly and doesn't keep a copy.

Device backups and transfers

Separately from the app, your device's operating system may include the app's data in its own backups and in transfers to a new device, depending on your settings:

  • iOS: iCloud Backup, backups made with a computer, and transfers to a new iPhone.
  • Android: Android's backup to your Google account, and device-to-device transfer. When you reinstall the app, Android may restore its data from such a backup.

These features are provided and controlled by Apple or Google, under your account and device settings and their privacy policies. We don't receive these backups and can't access them. The app doesn't guarantee that device backups happen or that data can be recovered from them. To keep your own copy, use Export data from time to time.

Deleting your information, and your choices

  • Settings → Reset all data permanently deletes all restaurants and dishes from the app on this device.
  • Deleting the app removes its storage from the device.
  • Backup files you exported, and device backups, are separate. Delete them where they're stored.

The app doesn't send us any of your information, so there's nothing from the app for us to delete and no consent to withdraw. If you've emailed us, see "If you contact us" for how we handle that correspondence.

If you contact us

Writing to us is optional and happens outside the app, from your own email app. If you email hello.popsicle.apps@gmail.com, we receive what your message contains: your email address, the name shown by your email account, your message and any attachments or details you choose to include, such as your device model.

  • We use it to answer you and to improve our apps. We don't sell it or share it for advertising.
  • Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so messages are stored and processed by Google under its own privacy policy.
  • We don't keep support messages longer than we reasonably need them. You can ask us to delete your messages from our mailbox, and we'll do so unless we need to keep them, for example to deal with an ongoing request or where the law requires it. Copies held by email providers, such as in your own mailbox or in their backups, are controlled by those providers.

Please don't send backup files or other personal information unless it's needed to solve your problem.

Third parties

The app shares no information with third parties. No analytics, advertising or other third-party services receive your data. The only outside services involved are your device's platform provider (Apple or Google, for app stores and operating-system features you choose to use), GitHub (hosting this website) and Google (our support mailbox, only if you email us), as described in this policy.

Children

The app isn't directed at children. The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

App stores

When you download or update the app from an app store, the store provider (for example Apple's App Store) may process information under its own privacy policy, for example download statistics, or crash reports if you've chosen to share them with developers. We don't control that processing.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, for example for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive visitor logs. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics, tracking or scripts.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that change how information is handled, such as accounts, sync, purchases, analytics or advertising, we'll update this policy and the in-app privacy information before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
