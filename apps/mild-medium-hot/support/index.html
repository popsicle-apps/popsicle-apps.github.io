Quick answers

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device, inside the app, by default. There's no account and no cloud sync. Copies exist elsewhere only if you export a backup, or if your device's own backup includes the app's data. See the privacy policy.

How do I keep a copy, or move my data to another device?

In the app, go to Settings → Export data and choose where to save or send the backup file, for example Files or iCloud Drive on iOS, or Files or Google Drive on Android. On the other device, use Settings → Import backup and pick the file.

Your device's own backup may also include the app's data, depending on your settings: iCloud Backup on iOS, or Android's backup to your Google account. The app can't guarantee that these backups exist or can be restored.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Reset all data. It asks twice before deleting. Exported backup files and device backups are separate: delete them where they're stored.

How do I change between Light and Dark?

Settings → Appearance: Light, Dark or Follow System (the default).
