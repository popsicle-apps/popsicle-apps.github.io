Mild, Medium, Hot

Remember your restaurants, your dishes and exactly how spicy you like them.

View on the App Store

What it does

  • Save restaurants and the dishes you've tried, with notes.
  • Pick your spice level on a 7-step scale, from no spice to extra hot, and note what to say when ordering.
  • Mark favorites and search all your dishes.
  • Light, Dark or Follow System appearance.

Private by design

Your restaurants and dishes are stored locally on your device by default. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You decide when to export a backup and where to keep it.

Platforms and availability

Mild, Medium, Hot is made for iOS and Android.

  • iOS: free on the App Store for iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later). Not currently available in the European Union or China mainland.
  • Android: not currently available on Google Play.
